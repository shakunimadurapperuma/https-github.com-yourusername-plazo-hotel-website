About Us

Ayubown! ( may you live long ) Welcome to palazo hotel.the largest family of luxury hotels in Sri Lanka has to offer! On the resplendent isle.we wish all our guests a long life.and even though some have called it paradise.we call it home.

A home filled with stories untold and wonders enchanted.which I invite unti step into and discover at our palazo hotel in Sri Lanka. For nearly half a century,we have been honoured to share the magic of our tropical land with the world.with humble beginnings on my family's favorite seaside town in negambo.it was my uncle,lalith wickrama.who set us in the path we still proudly follow today. Be it hidden amidst the mytique of historical cities.nestled away in rustic villages.cushioned warmly up in the mountain clouds,or pampered down by the setting sun of the Indian ocen- our doors are always open,whenever u may find urself in Sri Lanka. The world has graced oye shores for centuries,forgiving a beautiful relationship that has always held a special place in our hearts.so special in fact,that even though u may arrives as guests,u will leave as friends.but return as family.because when u stay with us.u stay at home Welcome to our family - we look forward to meeting you
